`Bone Assembly > Prepare Controller Hands` derives separate left/right triangle sets from the combined skinned mesh, retains the rig and bind poses, applies a blue surgical-glove material, and assigns the prefabs to BoneGameSettings. The idle Animator is removed from derived prefabs so it cannot override controller poses.

ControllerHandVisual contains editable open and closed local rotations for 15 finger joints. Index joints follow Trigger; other joints follow Grip. Verify wrist alignment and finger curl on Quest controllers.
